Privacy policy

In short

BarMint runs entirely in your browser. What you enter (names, IBANs, amounts, barcode content) is not sent to us or anyone else. There are no cookies, no tracking, no analytics and no external fonts.

Controller

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on Microsoft Azure (Static Web Apps). Content is delivered through Microsoft's worldwide network; the site is managed in the US region (East US 2). When you open it, Microsoft processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, time, requested file and browser identifier to deliver the page and protect it from abuse. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure operation). Microsoft acts as a processor under its Data Protection Addendum (DPA). Data is transferred to the USA in the process; Microsoft is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision, Art. 45 GDPR). We do not analyse these logs.

Your input

Form data stays in your browser. When you share a link, it is stored in the part of the address after “#”. Browsers never send that part to the server. Anyone who receives a shared link can see the data, so only share links with people who may see it.

Your language choice and the “Recent” list (up to 8 exported codes) are kept in your browser's localStorage, on your device only. This is required for features you use (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can remove the list at any time with “Clear” or by deleting the site data in your browser.
